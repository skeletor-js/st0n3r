## Overall

The manuscript has a genuinely strong architecture — the numbers (feet, dollars, square footage) function as a real narrative device, chapter 2's overheard county conversation pays off precisely in chapter 14's ruling, and Ruth's voice is controlled and consistent — but the prose leans hard on a small set of recurring devices (balanced antithesis, filter-word realizations, near-identical chapter-ending shapes, and at least one directly repeated simile) that flatten the second half into a uniform rhythm an editor would want thinned. More consequentially, because the book trains readers to trust its precise numbers and explicit setups, a couple of those numbers don't reconcile (the full-sun canopy figure reappearing as a live worry after it's resolved; the unexplained gap between the compliant acreage the math implies and the 8,000 sq ft the county actually grants), and two explicitly planted threads — the promised call to Corinne Yee and Denny's unrefuted argument — are never brought back for closure. None of this breaks the book, but a careful pass addressing the repeated imagery, the antithesis tic, and the two loose threads would meaningfully tighten it.
