# Motifs

> The recurring images, objects, phrases, and symbols the book runs on —
> the river that keeps returning, the cold hands, the word "ledger." Register
> each one here so the harness can track where it recurs, whether the ending
> rhymes with the opening, and flag echoes you may not have noticed.
>
> `anchors` are the concrete phrases to match on, semicolon-separated within
> the cell (e.g. `river; the current; downstream`). Matching is stem-aware,
> so `rivers` finds `river`. `meaning` is the intended weight — what the
> motif is *for*. Registering a motif never changes the prose; it only tells
> `stoner motifs scan` and `stoner motifs rhyme` what to look for. Nothing is
> auto-registered: you plant a motif here, by hand or by adopting a mined
> candidate (`stoner motifs candidates`).
